Snackerpillar

A very hungry caterpillar made of circles. Every dot he eats adds a new circle to his back, in that dot's color.

Level 1 1 / 10 circles

Grow long enough to finish each of the 3 levels. Use the arrow keys to move. On a touch screen, drag anywhere on the grass like a trackpad. Don't bump into rocks, mushrooms or his own body!
